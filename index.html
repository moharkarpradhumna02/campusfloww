Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers.

Clear insights into tracking, payments, OTP security, and our conversational AI buddy WAYZEN.

Campus Flow uses a 3-layer dead-reckoning pipeline based on the Newson & Krumm (2009) Hidden Markov Model map matcher and a 2D Kalman filter. When raw GPS jumps, has an accuracy radius >65m, or drops entirely (e.g. entering hostel tunnels or under dense trees), the engine automatically falls back to an exponential decay model: \(w = e^{-\Delta t / 90}\), blending instantaneous speed with historical p85 segment travel times. This guarantees 100% survival over 120-second dropouts with Mean Absolute Error under 90 seconds!

You have 3 easy payment options:
• Instant UPI QR: Click "Pay / Buy Pass", scan the onboard Google Pay QR code with any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), and your ride pass is validated instantly.
• Campus Flow Wallet: Tap the Wallet button on top, recharge via UPI or Debit card, and pay rides in 1-click even with poor mobile internet.
• Cash on Board: Pay exact cash to the vehicle driver upon boarding; the driver will verify your 4-digit OTP to confirm the trip.

During peak campus hours (e.g. 8:30 AM lectures and 5:00 PM hostel return), unverified boarding creates chaos. When you book a ride or pass, Campus Flow generates a secure 4-digit OTP (e.g. 8392). When the vehicle arrives, share this OTP with driver Rajesh Sharma or Vikram Singh or type it in the Driver Chat window. The trip officially activates only once the driver confirms the OTP, ensuring only authorized students occupy reserved seats.

In the Payment modal, enter the promo coupon code CAMPUS10 to get an instant 10% discount on any ride or semester pass. For your very first trip of the academic term, use coupon FREEFIRST for a 100% free ride. You can also ask WAYZEN AI: "WAYZEN, best coupon batao" and it will apply it for you in one click!
